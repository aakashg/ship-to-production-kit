# Experiment: concierge weekly digest

Status: NOT RUN (headless session, no product, data, or email access in this repo).

## Run it
1. Choose 10 to 20 active dashboard users who agreed to take part. Choose a matched group of similar users who do not receive anything.
2. For 3 consecutive Mondays, hand-build the email: top 3 to 5 numbers from their dashboard, change vs previous week, one link back. Send from a normal mailbox, with a plain-text "reply STOP to cancel" line. Do not include anyone else's data.
3. Record per recipient: opened, clicked back, replied, dashboard visits that week (and for the matched group).
4. Week 3 survey, one question: "How would you feel if this stopped?" (very / somewhat / not disappointed).

## Kill criteria
- Fewer than 3 of 10 recipients open in weeks 2 and 3.
- Visits fall versus the matched group.
- Fewer than half answer "very" or "somewhat" disappointed.

## Cost
About 1 hour per week for 3 weeks, plus 1 day to recruit. No code, no deploy.
